## Observations (hand-checked)

- `GET /albums/{id}`: `popularity` is a real but unreliable album-level number (A ROCK by HARDY returned `2` while its tracks are ~40-70). The app derives album world heat from the average of its tracks' `popularity` instead.
- Album tracks carry `linked_from.id` (the original track id) when relinked. Your top lists may use either id, so heat checks both.
- `available_markets` is `[]` and `genres` is `[]` on this album; `preview_url` is always `null`.
